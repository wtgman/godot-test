## How you are assessed
There are three assessment tasks. You must complete all three to be deemed competent.
Task | What you do | When
Task 1: Knowledge questions | Short written answers on grief responses and your role | Week 3
Task 2: Case study report | Plan the support for a family in a written case study | Week 6
Task 3: Placement observation | Your supervisor observes a conversation with a family member | Week 8
Each task is marked satisfactory or not yet satisfactory. You can resubmit a task once.
