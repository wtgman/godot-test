Text version of this activity
What cannot wait?
=================

What this shows: A prediction activity. The learner answers a question before the answer is shown, then sees the answer and the reasoning.
How it is arranged: Question first, then the learner's answer, then the reveal.

Contents:
  - Question: Three things a family member says to you on one shift. Which one goes to your supervisor straight away, not at the end of the shift?
  - Option 1: "Mum's cardigan has gone missing from the laundry again.". Worth passing on, but it can wait for the end of the shift.
  - Option 2: "Dad keeps saying he doesn't want to go on any more.". Right. Any mention of not wanting to go on goes to your supervisor immediately.
  - Option 3: "When is the doctor next visiting?". Find out or pass it on, but it is not urgent.
  - Some things cannot wait: "Dad keeps saying he doesn't want to go on any more.".
  - Why: Any mention of self-harm, of not wanting to go on, or of a risk to someone else goes to your supervisor immediately, in person. Everything else still gets passed on, the same shift.
