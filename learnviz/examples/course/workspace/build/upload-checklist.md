# Upload checklist: Supporting People Through Grief and Loss

4 activities built into 4 pages. 3 pages skipped.

## Steps

1. **Try it in a sandbox course first.** In a blank course, go to Settings, then Import Course Content, choose Canvas Course Export Package, and upload `supporting-people-through-grief-and-loss-with-activities.imscc`. Canvas imports the whole course with each activity already on its page, and the figures as course files.
2. **Then bring it into the live course, knowing how Canvas matches content.** Re-importing into the course the export came from updates pages that have the same identifier, and any edits made there since the export are overwritten. Export the live course again just before you build, so nothing is lost.
3. **Upload each SCORM package** in `scorm/` with the SCORM tool, if your course has it, choosing graded or ungraded, and add it to the module next to its page. Then delete the yellow teacher note on that page.
4. **Resolve the 4 drafts.** Each has a yellow draft note on its page and a review sheet in its folder under `activities/`. Build again with `--release` once they are checked.
5. **Check in Student View**: open each changed page, answer an activity, open a SCORM activity and look at the gradebook.
