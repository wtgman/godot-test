### Text version

```
Which response is this?
=======================

What this shows: A sorting activity: 9 examples, each to be placed in one of 5 categories.
How it is arranged: Categories: Denial, Anger, Bargaining, Depression, Acceptance. Which response does each of these show most clearly?

Contents:
  - "The scan must have been someone else's. These things get mixed up all the time.": Denial. It rejects the information itself. The cue is disbelief about the facts, rather than distress about them.
  - "Why did it take three months for anyone to send him for a scan?": Anger. The blame points outward, at the system. It accepts the diagnosis and is furious about how it arrived.
  - "If I get him walking every day and off the sugar, that will buy him more time, won't it?": Bargaining. A deal: if I do this, then that. It sounds practical and hopeful, which is exactly why it is easy to miss.
  - "I don't see the point in getting dressed any more.": Depression. Loss of meaning in ordinary things. Listen, and pass it on to your supervisor. This is where you watch for risk.
  - "I want to sit down with the kids this weekend and talk about what he would want.": Acceptance. Planning around the reality rather than against it. Acceptance is not happiness. It is engaging with what is.
  - "You people are useless. Nobody tells us anything.": Anger. Aimed at the person in the room. It is almost never about that person, so it is worth not taking it personally.
  - "I keep thinking if I had made him go to the doctor sooner, none of this would be happening.": Bargaining. "If only" looks backwards, but it is the same move as a deal: searching for the lever that would have changed the outcome. It often comes with guilt.
  - "He's fine. He was up and about this morning. He just needs a good rest.": Denial. The evidence of the morning is used to push away the diagnosis. Denial often protects someone while the news sinks in.
  - "I haven't been sleeping. I just sit in his room.": Depression. Withdrawal, and a sign of how much this is costing them. Worth mentioning to the team so someone checks on the family as well as the resident.
```
