Passing on a concern ====================

What this shows: An ordering activity: put 6 steps in the right order, then check. How it is arranged: A family member tells you something that worries you. Put these steps in the order you would take them.

Contents: - Step 1: Listen without interrupting. You cannot pass on accurately what you did not hear in full. - Step 2: Acknowledge what they have said. It tells them they were heard before anything else happens. - Step 3: Tell them you will pass it on, and to whom. They should never be surprised that someone else knows. - Step 4: Tell your supervisor the same shift, in person. A note alone can sit unread. In person, the same shift, it gets acted on. - Step 5: Record it in the progress notes. The record comes after the conversation with your supervisor, as your service requires. - Step 6: Check back with the family that someone has followed up. Closing the loop is what the family remembers, and it catches the follow-ups that did not happen.
