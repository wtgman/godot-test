Steps

  1. Try it in a sandbox course first. Canvas imports the whole course with each activity already on its page, and the figures as course files.
  2. Then bring it into the live course, knowing how Canvas matches content. Re-importing into the course the export came from updates pages that have the same identifier, and any edits made there since the export are overwritten. Export the live course again just before you build, so nothing is lost.
