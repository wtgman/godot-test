# Research notes: the Instagram acquisition

Gathered by web search on 28/09/2026. Every figure below comes from a search
result that states it. **The pages themselves were not opened** (the research
environment blocked direct page fetches), so quotes are from search summaries.
Open the primary URLs and confirm the wording before release.

Confidence is high where two independent sources agree, medium where one
reputable source reports it.

## What does not exist

- **Instagram's profit.** Meta reports two segments, Family of Apps and Reality
  Labs, and does not break out individual apps. [Meta 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)
- **Instagram's revenue, officially.** The widely repeated "about US$20bn of ad
  revenue in 2019" traces back to a single [Bloomberg report](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-02-04/instagram-generates-more-than-a-quarter-of-facebook-s-sales)
  citing people familiar with the matter. It is a leak, not a disclosure, and
  must be labelled that way if used.
