The Instagram acquisition, in four numbers
==========================================

What this shows: A panel of 4 large figures, each in its own tile.
How it is arranged: 4 tiles in a grid 2 across, read left to right then down.

Contents:
  - $1bn: Paid by Facebook in April 2012. In US dollars, for a company that had never charged anyone anything.
  - 13: Employees at the time of sale. About 77 million US dollars per head.
  - $0: Revenue at the time of sale. No advertising, no subscriptions, no marketplace.
  - 18: Months from launch to acquisition. October 2010 to April 2012.

The price was not paid for the business. It was paid for the audience, and for stopping a competitor owning it. That is the point of the lesson, and it is why revenue is the most useful number on this panel.

Source: Company announcements, April 2012. Figures also used in the accompanying timeline. Verify against your unit's prescribed text before use.
