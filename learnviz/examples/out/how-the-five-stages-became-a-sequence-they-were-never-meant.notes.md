# How the five stages became a sequence they were never meant to be
**Learning intent.** Learners can explain what Kübler-Ross actually claimed, and why describing grief as five ordered stages misleads both workers and families.
The model describes five responses. Almost everything that went wrong with it came from reading them as steps.
