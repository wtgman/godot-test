Walk through it step by step

The deadlines are already on the chart: site work cannot start before week 6, and handover is at week 12. Watch what has to fit between them.

  1. The council permit takes six weeks, and nothing on site can start without it. This is the critical path. Everything else is arranged around it.
  2. Soil testing runs inside the permit window. It does not hold anything up, so it has slack.
  3. Volunteer recruitment also runs in parallel, finishing exactly as the permit arrives.
  4. Induction cannot start until recruitment closes, and must finish before anyone is on site.
  5. Site clearing starts the day the permit lands. Any delay to the permit pushes this, and everything after it.
  6. The raised beds follow the clearing.
  7. Irrigation overlaps the bed build on purpose: the plumber is only available in week 9.
  8. Paths go down while the beds are finished.
  9. Planting day lands in week 11, one week before handover. Ask the room: which single task, if it slipped by a week, would push the handover?
