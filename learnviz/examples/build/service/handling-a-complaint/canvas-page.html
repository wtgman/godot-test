Put it in order: Handling a complaint

 

In what order should you handle a customer complaint?

These steps are out of order. Write down the order you think is right, then open the box to check.

  • Agree what you will do, and by when
  • Acknowledge it and apologise for the experience
  • Follow up afterwards
  • Listen without interrupting
  • Ask what would put it right

 

Check the order
  1. Listen without interrupting. People need to be heard before they can hear you. Jumping in with a fix usually makes it take longer.
  2. Acknowledge it and apologise for the experience. Apologising for how it felt is not admitting fault. It puts you on their side of the problem.
  3. Ask what would put it right. The fix you assume they want is often not the one they do.
  4. Agree what you will do, and by when. A specific action and a time is what turns an apology into a plan.
  5. Follow up afterwards. Checking back is what customers remember, and it catches the fixes that did not work.
