Walk through it step by step

Three columns: what you might hear, what the person may need, and what not to say. The rows fill in one at a time. The order of the rows is the order Kübler-Ross listed them in, and nothing more.

  1. Denial. The information itself is pushed away. What helps is time: offer it again later rather than pressing it now.
  2. Anger. Often aimed at whoever is in the room, which on placement may be you. It is rarely about you, so let it be heard rather than defending anyone.
  3. Bargaining. If I do this, then that, or if only. It sounds hopeful and practical, which is why it is easy to miss. Be honest without taking the hope away.
  4. Depression. Withdrawal and loss of meaning. Be present rather than trying to fix it, and this is the one where you watch for risk and tell your supervisor.
  5. Acceptance. Not happiness, but planning around what is real. Support the person to act on their own decisions. And remember: these five can come in any order, and come back.
