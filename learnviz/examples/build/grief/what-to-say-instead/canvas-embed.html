What to say instead ===================

What this shows: A set of 7 recall cards. How it is arranged: What could you say instead?

Contents: - "You need to accept this.": "This is a lot to take in." It acknowledges the shock without telling them what they should be feeling. - "At least he had a long life.": "Tell me about him." Any sentence that starts with "at least" measures their loss for them. - "I know how you feel.": "I can't imagine what this is like for you." Nobody knows exactly, and saying so invites them to tell you. - "Everything happens for a reason.": "I'm so sorry." Simple is almost always better than an explanation. - "You should be over this by now.": "How are you going with it all?" Grief keeps no timetable, and a check-in is better than a deadline. - "Stay strong for the family.": "It's alright not to be okay." Asking someone to be strong asks them to hide. - "There's no point getting angry.": "I can hear how angry you are." Naming the anger lets it be said, which usually lowers it.
