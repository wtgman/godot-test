How Instagram grew
==================

What this shows: A step-by-step walkthrough of a diagram, in 5 steps.
How it is arranged: The parts appear in this order: October 2010, April 2012, June 2013, August 2016, August 2020.

Contents:
  - Step 1: October 2010. Instagram launches on iPhone only. Filters made phone photos look good on slow connections, which is what people needed at the time.
  - Step 2: April 2012. Eighteen months later, Facebook agrees to buy it. Notice the gap: eighteen months from launch to a billion-dollar deal.
  - Step 3: June 2013. Video arrives, a direct response to Vine. This is the first of three times Instagram answers a competitor by adding the competitor's feature.
  - Step 4: August 2016. Stories, copied openly from Snapchat. The second time.
  - Step 5: August 2020. Reels, answering TikTok. The third time. Ask the room: what does that pattern tell you about how platforms compete?
