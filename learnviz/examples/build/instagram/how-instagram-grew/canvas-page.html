Walk through it step by step
  1. October 2010. Instagram launches on iPhone only. Filters made phone photos look good on slow connections, which is what people needed at the time.
  2. April 2012. Eighteen months later, Facebook agrees to buy it. Notice the gap: eighteen months from launch to a billion-dollar deal.
  3. June 2013. Video arrives, a direct response to Vine. This is the first of three times Instagram answers a competitor by adding the competitor's feature.
  4. August 2016. Stories, copied openly from Snapchat. The second time.
  5. August 2020. Reels, answering TikTok. The third time. Ask the room: what does that pattern tell you about how platforms compete?
