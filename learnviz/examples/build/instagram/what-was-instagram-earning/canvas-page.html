Predict: What was Instagram earning?

 

Before you read on, decide your answer. Write it down or say it out loud. Then open the box below to check.

When Facebook agreed to pay about US$1 billion for Instagram, how much revenue was Instagram making?

  1. Nothing at all
  2. About US$10 million a year
  3. About US$100 million a year
  4. About US$1 billion a year

 

Decided? Open to check: Nothing. So what was Facebook buying?

Answer: Nothing at all

Instagram had no revenue, no advertising and thirteen staff. What Facebook paid for was about 30 million users, a team that had built something people used every day, and the certainty that a competitor would not own it.

  • When a company has no revenue, its price is a bet about the future, not a calculation about the present.
  • Buying a company partly to stop a rival buying it is a strategy with a name: a defensive acquisition.

About each option:

  • Nothing at all. Right. Instagram had never charged anyone for anything, and ran no advertising.
  • About US$10 million a year. A reasonable guess for a popular app, but Instagram had no revenue at all.
  • About US$100 million a year. That would put the price at ten times revenue, a common rule of thumb. The real answer makes the rule of thumb useless.
  • About US$1 billion a year. That would make the deal look cheap. In fact Instagram earned nothing.
