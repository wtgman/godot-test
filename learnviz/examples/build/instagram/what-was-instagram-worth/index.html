A price is only readable next to other prices

Instagram was announced at about US$1 billion, less than YouTube six years earlier, for a company with 13 staff and no revenue. Two years later WhatsApp was announced at nineteen times that. And Tumblr, bought for almost exactly Instagram's price, went the other way entirely.

  • Announced prices are headlines. The Instagram deal was mostly Facebook shares, which fell after Facebook's float, so it was worth about US$715 million by the time it closed.
  • WhatsApp went the opposite way: US$19 billion announced, about US$22 billion by the close.
  • Yahoo later wrote off most of what it paid for Tumblr, and it was reportedly sold in 2019 for about US$3 million. Same price as Instagram, opposite outcome.
