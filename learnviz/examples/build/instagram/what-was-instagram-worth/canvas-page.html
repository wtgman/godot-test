Estimate: What was Instagram worth?

 

How much did Facebook announce it would pay for Instagram in 2012, and for WhatsApp in 2014?

You know these:

  • YouTube: US$1.65bn billion US dollars, announced
  • Tumblr: US$1.1bn billion US dollars, announced
  • LinkedIn: US$26.2bn billion US dollars, announced

Write down your estimate for Instagram and WhatsApp before you open the box.

 

Guessed? Open to see a price is only readable next to other prices
  • YouTube: US$1.65bn billion US dollars, announced. Google, 2006
  • Instagram: US$1bn billion US dollars, announced. Facebook, 2012
  • Tumblr: US$1.1bn billion US dollars, announced. Yahoo, 2013
  • WhatsApp: US$19bn billion US dollars, announced. Facebook, 2014
  • LinkedIn: US$26.2bn billion US dollars, announced. Microsoft, 2016

Instagram was announced at about US$1 billion, less than YouTube six years earlier, for a company with 13 staff and no revenue. Two years later WhatsApp was announced at nineteen times that. And Tumblr, bought for almost exactly Instagram's price, went the other way entirely.

  • Announced prices are headlines. The Instagram deal was mostly Facebook shares, which fell after Facebook's float, so it was worth about US$715 million by the time it closed.
  • WhatsApp went the opposite way: US$19 billion announced, about US$22 billion by the close.
  • Yahoo later wrote off most of what it paid for Tumblr, and it was reportedly sold in 2019 for about US$3 million. Same price as Instagram, opposite outcome.
