Where the marks come from in this unit
======================================

What this shows: A grid of 100 small squares, 10 across, where each square is one mark.
How it is arranged: Filled from the top left, going left to right then down, one category after another in the order listed in the key.

Contents:
  - Practical observation: 50 of 100 marks, which is 50 per cent. Two observed sessions in the simulated workplace, 25 marks each.
  - Portfolio of evidence: 30 of 100 marks, which is 30 per cent. Submitted in week 10. Marked against the four criteria in the rubric.
  - Knowledge quiz: 20 of 100 marks, which is 20 per cent. Week 6, in class, open book.

Half the marks are in the practical, so a strong portfolio cannot rescue a missed observation. Note also that the quiz alone will not carry you to the 50 marks needed to pass.

Source: Example weighting for layout review. Replace with the weighting in your own unit guide before publishing.
